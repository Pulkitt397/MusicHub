1. PROJECT PURPOSE & CORE PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: MusicHub is a quiet, native Android control surface that helps a user 
  see and control music playing in supported music apps (Spotify, YouTube Music, 
  Amazon Music), without pretending to replace those apps or stream their audio.
- Non-Goals: No local audio playback/decoding/streaming, no fake recommendations,
  no scraping private endpoints, no credential collection, no cloud backend.
- Source Truth: Displays only capabilities actually exposed by the active media 
  session (play/pause, next, prev, seek).
- Design System: Pure, calm utility. Solid surfaces (#101214, #181B1E, #202428, 
  #2B3035), accent #A8C7B8, text #F2F4F3 / #A7AFB5 / #777F86. No gradients, 
  no blur decoration, no glassmorphism, no fake recommendation cards.
